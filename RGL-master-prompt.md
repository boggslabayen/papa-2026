# RGL Website Master Prompt

## Prompt

Create a premium personal brand website for **Robert Labayen (RGL)**.

Robert Labayen is a creative genius, creative leader, artist, motivational speaker, songwriter, and painter. He has established himself in the advertising and creative industry in the Philippines. The website's purpose is to introduce who Robert is, communicate the services he offers, and make visitors feel that they can confidently hire him for creative workshops, talks, and speaking engagements.

The key takeaway for users should be:

> Robert Labayen is someone I can trust to inspire, guide, and elevate my team's creativity, leadership, communication, and brand thinking.

The site should also support weekly blog or journal entries written by Robert, and it must include a clear Contact / Book Us section for inquiries.

## Creative Peg

Use [The Art of Vision](https://www.theartofvision.com/) as a strategic peg for flow and conversion logic, not as a visual copy.

Take inspiration from:

- Strong personal-brand hero section
- Clear authority and credibility early on
- Signature speaking and workshop offers presented clearly
- A polished, high-end, inspiring feel
- Strong call-to-action paths toward booking

However, the RGL website should have its own identity.

It should feel:

- Cleaner
- Calmer
- More editorial
- More refined

Avoid flashy effects, clutter, excessive animation, or overly corporate visuals.

## Overall Brand Feeling

The website should feel:

- Clean
- Elegant
- Artistic
- Credible
- Inspirational
- Thoughtful
- Premium
- Human
- Editorial

## Audience

The website is for:

- Corporate teams
- Brand and communications teams
- Leaders and managers
- Schools and organizations
- Creative professionals
- Companies looking for workshops, keynote talks, or inspiration sessions

## Primary Goals

1. Introduce Robert Labayen clearly and memorably.
2. Establish his credibility as a creative leader, artist, and speaker.
3. Present his talks and workshops in a compelling, easy-to-understand way.
4. Encourage visitors to inquire about booking Robert.
5. Create a platform for Robert's weekly blog or journal writing.

## Recommended Pages

- Home
- About Robert
- Talks and Workshops
- Blog or Journal
- Book Robert or Contact

## Homepage Structure

### 1. Hero Section

- Strong, memorable headline introducing Robert Labayen
- Short supporting paragraph explaining who he is and what he brings
- Primary CTA: `Book a Workshop` or `Book Robert`
- Secondary CTA: `Explore Talks` or `Read the Journal`

### 2. Credibility and Authority Section

- Short proof points about Robert's background and impact
- Position him as a respected creative leader in the Philippine advertising and creative industry
- Include artist, speaker, songwriter, and painter identity in a refined way

### 3. Signature Talks and Workshops Section

Feature Robert's core workshop and speaking topics:

- `Rapid Creative`: Techniques for Quick Idea Generation
- `Leading with Meaning`: Leadership that Inspires with Vision and Purpose
- `Show and Tell Level Up`: Presentations that Connect and Persuade
- `Team One`: The Culture of Cohesion and Inspiration
- `Max Mode`: Breaking Your Own Records
- `Captivate`: Communication, Persuasion, and Infatuation

For each topic, include:

- Title
- Short summary
- Intended audience
- Key outcomes
- Available format: keynote, 1-hour talk, or extended workshop

### 4. About Robert Section

- A concise but emotionally resonant introduction to Robert
- Communicate both mastery and humanity
- Highlight his depth in creativity, leadership, communication, branding, and artistic practice
- Make him feel multi-dimensional, not just a speaker

### 5. Who This Is For Section

- Show which kinds of people and organizations benefit from working with Robert
- Help visitors quickly self-identify

### 6. Outcomes and Value Section

Focus on what participants or clients gain from Robert's talks and workshops, such as:

- Stronger creativity
- Better communication
- More inspiring leadership
- Improved presentation skills
- Stronger team culture
- Clearer brand and messaging thinking
- Greater self-belief and motivation

### 7. Blog or Journal Preview

- Feature recent or selected journal entries by Robert
- Make this section feel thoughtful and literary, not like a generic corporate blog
- Invite readers into Robert's mind, ideas, and reflections

### 8. Final CTA and Booking Section

- Clear invitation to book Robert for talks, workshops, and creative engagements
- Include inquiry form
- Include space for contact information
- Tone should feel warm, personal, and professional

## Page Directions

### About Page

- Tell Robert's story with clarity and elegance
- Balance credibility with personality
- Include his roles as creative leader, artist, songwriter, painter, and motivational speaker
- Show how his career and life perspective shape the experience he brings to audiences and teams

### Talks and Workshops Page

- Present Robert's offers as premium, thoughtful experiences
- Make the page easy to scan but rich in meaning
- Each workshop should feel practical, inspiring, and transformative
- Reinforce that these can be tailored for organizations, teams, schools, or leadership groups

### Blog or Journal Page

- Design for weekly publishing
- Use a clean reading layout with excellent typography
- Give it a strong editorial feel
- Support featured post, article list, and category or tag structure if needed later
- Make it CMS-ready for future publishing workflows

### Book Robert or Contact Page

- Include a clean inquiry form
- Include fields suitable for speaking and workshop inquiries
- Suggested fields: name, organization, email, event type, audience size, preferred date, message
- Make the CTA feel premium and inviting, not salesy

## Visual Direction

- Clean and spacious layout
- Premium editorial style
- Strong typography hierarchy
- Generous white space
- Minimal but thoughtful animation
- Refined image handling
- Use photography, portraits, artwork, or texture only when they add meaning
- No clutter
- No generic motivational-speaker aesthetic
- No trendy startup look
- The site should feel timeless and distinctive

## Typography Direction

- Use `Bricolage Grotesque` for hero text, page titles, and section headers
- Use `Plus Jakarta Sans` for body copy, navigation, buttons, forms, and general interface text
- Use `Space Mono` for footnotes, captions, dates, labels, metadata, and small editorial details

Typography should create a strong editorial hierarchy and help the website feel premium, authored, and visually intelligent.

## Image Direction

Use the supplied Robert Labayen PNG portrait assets as the primary visual system of the website. These images should appear across the site in a curated, editorial way and should help express Robert's personality: thoughtful, warm, creative, confident, and inspiring.

Do not treat the images like generic speaker headshots.

Use them as art-directed storytelling elements.

### How to Use the Images

- Use a strong portrait as the homepage hero image
- Use alternate portraits across About, Talks and Workshops, Blog, and Contact sections
- Mix close-up, mid-shot, and full-body portraits for rhythm and variety
- Use expressive poses to support section meaning
- Use the more open, welcoming, or gesture-based images near CTAs and booking sections
- Use the more thoughtful or composed portraits in About and editorial sections
- Use images with stronger personality or movement for workshop and speaking sections
- Use the pink blazer and white blazer looks strategically to create visual variety across the site

### Visual Treatment of the Images

- Keep the presentation clean and premium
- Integrate the PNG portraits into spacious layouts with strong typography
- Let the portraits breathe with generous white space
- Avoid cluttered collage treatment
- Avoid cheesy drop shadows or loud graphic effects
- If needed, use subtle framing, soft gradients, or minimal editorial composition devices to unify the PNG cutouts into the page design
- The portraits should feel intentional, elegant, and modern

### Suggested Placement Logic

- `Home hero`: one of the strongest confident portraits, ideally a clean three-quarter or standing pose
- `About page`: thoughtful, composed, close-up or mid-shot portraits
- `Talks and Workshops`: dynamic or expressive portraits that suggest energy, communication, and presence
- `Blog or Journal`: quieter, more reflective portraits
- `Contact / Book Robert`: welcoming or gesture-forward portraits that feel invitational

Important:

The website should feel designed around Robert himself. The supplied portraits should make the experience feel personal, authored, and distinctive. The images are not secondary assets; they are a core part of the brand presence.

## Tone of Copy

The writing should feel:

- Intelligent
- Warm
- Reflective
- Confident
- Human
- Inspiring without sounding cliched
- Creative but not self-indulgent
- Professional but not stiff

## Important Writing Direction

Do not make the site sound exaggerated, overly promotional, or generic.

Avoid empty phrases like "unlock your full potential" unless grounded in Robert's actual point of view.

The copy should feel authored, thoughtful, and true to a seasoned creative mind.

## Functional Requirements

- Fully responsive on desktop and mobile
- Clear navigation
- Strong CTA visibility
- CMS-ready blog structure
- Contact and booking form
- Scalable structure for future additions such as testimonials, media features, or gallery

## Final Outcome

The website should feel like a premium, clean, highly intentional digital home for Robert Labayen.

It should present him as a respected creative authority whose work sits at the intersection of:

- Creativity
- Leadership
- Communication
- Branding
- Self-development
- Art

Visitors should leave understanding:

- Who Robert is
- What he offers
- Why they should book him for a workshop, talk, or creative engagement
